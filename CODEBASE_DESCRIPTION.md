# MoneyMoneyMoney

MoneyMoneyMoney is a Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 mod that adds physical currency, persistent player balances, banking tools, a banker, bank-themed building blocks, and generated bank structures.

## Features

- Redeemable $1, $5, $10, $20, and $50 bills
- Persistent wallet and bank balances
- Deposits, withdrawals, and transfers between online players
- A Money Manager GUI opened through a command or by interacting with a Banker
- A Banker entity and spawn egg
- Bank Tiles, Vault Plating, Teller Glass, a Bank Computer, and a usable Bank Seat
- Bank village, outpost, and mansion world-generation structures

Using a bill consumes it and adds its face value to the player's wallet. Creative-mode players can redeem bills without consuming them.

## Commands

- `/money balance` — show your wallet and bank balances
- `/walletbalance` — compatibility alias for `/money balance`
- `/money menu` — open the Money Manager
- `/money deposit <amount>` — move wallet funds into the bank
- `/money withdraw <amount>` — move bank funds into the wallet
- `/money transfer <player> <amount>` — send wallet funds to an online player
- `/money mint <amount>` — mint bills using the fewest denominations (operator level 2 required)

All balance-changing actions are validated and performed by the server. Player balances are stored in a Forge capability and copied when the player entity is recreated.
