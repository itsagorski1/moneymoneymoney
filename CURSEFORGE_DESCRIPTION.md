# MoneyMoneyMoney

Bring physical cash and banking to Minecraft.

MoneyMoneyMoney adds redeemable $1, $5, $10, $20, and $50 bills. Turn bills into wallet funds, store money safely in your bank balance, or transfer funds to another online player through the Money Manager.

## Features

- Five usable bill denominations
- Persistent wallet and bank balances
- Deposits, withdrawals, and player-to-player transfers
- Money Manager banking screen
- Interactive Banker entity and spawn egg
- Bank Tiles, Vault Plating, Teller Glass, Bank Computer, and usable Bank Seat
- Bank village, outpost, and mansion structures

## Commands

- `/money balance` — view wallet and bank balances
- `/walletbalance` — alternate balance command
- `/money menu` — open the Money Manager
- `/money deposit <amount>`
- `/money withdraw <amount>`
- `/money transfer <player> <amount>`
- `/money mint <amount>` — operator-only bill minting

Built for Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.
